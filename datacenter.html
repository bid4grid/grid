Data Center Power Architecture & Sizing Guide

How it fits: A distribution feeder supplies the site transformers, then switchgear distributes power to the UPS and other loads. This calculator is independent of the Distribution CAD cascade; their inputs are not synchronized.

Load inputs: IT/rack is connected load per rack, and IT demand is its expected percentage. Include cooling and other equipment outside the UPS in the non-UPS load. For this simplified model, include any UPS-supported cooling in the IT load input. UPS efficiency is at load; UPS PF is its output power factor, and facility PF is measured at the transformer.

Battery: Enter usable DC energy after reserves and derating; battery efficiency is the battery-to-load efficiency. Runtime assumes the shared battery supports the full IT load only; generator start and recharge are excluded.

Capacity and redundancy: The model assumes balanced three-phase loads and identical units sharing the load. N−1 checks only whether the remaining units have enough kVA after one fails; it does not verify UPS kW limits, redundant paths or availability.

Design limits: The diagram is conceptual. Actual A/B paths, generators, transfer equipment, switchgear ratings, switching and protective coordination are not modeled. Estimates also exclude transformer losses, harmonics, battery discharge-rate effects, equipment kW limits and generator capacity. Have qualified engineers and manufacturers verify the design and equipment ratings.
